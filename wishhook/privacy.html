WishHook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

WishHook is a business networking app that shows professionals around you, in a list, on a radar and in augmented reality. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. WishHook is published on the App Store by Viktoriia Sulzhyk ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code or link.
  • Profile data you choose to add: name, photo, role, company, tagline, what you are looking for and what you offer, open roles, LinkedIn link, country and city, and date of birth (used only for matching).
  • Voice intro: a short audio recording, if you record one.
  • Location: your device location while you use the app, to show you people nearby and to show them the distance to you. We never show your exact position to other users, only an approximate distance.
  • Messages: the chats you have with other users, and photos you send in chats.
  • Identifiers: your account ID and a push notification token for your device.
  • Nearby Interaction (Ultra Wideband): on supported iPhones, a temporary session token is exchanged with nearby WishHook users to measure distance and direction. It is not stored.

What we do not collect

The camera is used only for the live AR view. We do not record, store or upload camera images. We do not use advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use data

Only to run the app: create your account, show your card to people nearby, match you with relevant people, deliver messages and notifications, keep the service safe (reports, blocking, moderation) and fix problems.

Who can see your data

Other WishHook users can see your public profile card and approximate distance while you are On Air. Only the people you chat with can see your messages. Our infrastructure providers process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Expo / Apple (push notifications). We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, Settings, Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photos, voice intro, likes and messages. You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to its processing. Contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

Age

WishHook is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and protected with access rules so that users can only reach what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sharing in a business context.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

How do I sign in?

Enter your email on the sign-in screen. We send you a sign-in link and code. Open the email on the same iPhone and tap the link, or type the code. No passwords needed.

Why don't I see anyone nearby?

Make sure Location is allowed for WishHook in iOS Settings, then widen the distance on the Radar or On Air screen. People appear only while they are On Air.

How does the AR view work?

Open the AR tab and slowly move your phone around. Cards appear over the direction of people nearby. On iPhone 11 and newer, when two WishHook users are close, Ultra Wideband points you to the exact person.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap Report. We review reports and remove people who break the rules.

How do I delete my account?

Profile, Settings, Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data. You can also email us and we will do it for you.
